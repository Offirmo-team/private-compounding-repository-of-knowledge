CEMs "Commercial Electronic Messages" = emails, texts, instant messages, group messages and even app/website notifications when they are commercial
CEMs -- consent -- express = Ticking a box on a form or website. Signing up to receive newsletters or offers. Verbally agreeing during a call or in person (if documented appropriately). Selecting preferences in a digital platform or app. Agreeing to terms and conditions which contain a provision regarding consent to receive CEMs.
CEMs -- consent = express or inferred
CEMs -- not = Avoid images (esp. lifestyle/promotional) and use simple logos. Use neutral (not positive/persuasive) language about the Group. Avoid calls to action that encourage customers to use Group products or services. Messages containing factual instructions for customers may be permitted. Avoid links and URLs.
CEMs -- SPAM compliance = consent of the recipient, accurate sender identification and a simple way to unsubscribe
CEMs = offers, advertises, or promotes a product, service, or supplier, and includes promotional language, imagery, or links
